# Business Objective

The goal of this project is to predict whether a bank customer will subscribe to a term deposit after a marketing campaign.

The target variable is `y`:

- `yes`: Customer subscribed
- `no`: Customer did not subscribe

The model can help the bank identify likely subscribers and focus marketing efforts more efficiently.

Because the `yes` class is smaller, model performance will be evaluated using precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, and the confusion matrix in addition to accuracy.
